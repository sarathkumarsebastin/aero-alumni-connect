ABOUT THE NETWORK

A stronger connection beyond graduation

Aero Alumni Connect is designed to create a structured and sustainable relationship between the Department of Aerospace Engineering and its alumni community.

👥

Alumni Network

Maintain meaningful professional connections among graduates working across aerospace, aviation, defence, space, research, academia, and allied industries.

🎓

Student Engagement

Enable students to interact with alumni for mentoring, career guidance, projects, internships, higher studies, and professional development.

🚀

Industry Connect

Build stronger links with aerospace, aviation, defence, space, manufacturing, technology, and research organisations.

💡

Knowledge Exchange

Encourage knowledge sharing through technical talks, research collaboration, project guidance, innovation, entrepreneurship, and professional interaction.

CONNECT

Opportunities to contribute and collaborate

Alumni can remain actively connected with the Department through multiple forms of academic, professional, and community engagement.

🤝

Mentorship

Guide students in academics, careers, industry preparation, higher studies, research, and professional development.

🏢

Industry Opportunities

Share relevant internships, placements, projects, employment opportunities, and industry interactions.

🔬

Research Collaboration

Develop research connections between alumni, faculty, students, laboratories, universities, research organisations, and industry.

🎤

Guest Lectures

Invite experienced alumni to share technical knowledge, industry experience, emerging technologies, and professional insights.

🛠️

Project Guidance

Support student projects, engineering design, innovation, technical reviews, competitions, and practical problem solving.

🌐

Global Alumni Community

Build a connected alumni community across cities, countries, universities, industries, research organisations, and professional sectors.

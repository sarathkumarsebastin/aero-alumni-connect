WHY THIS PORTAL MATTERS

A practical and professional alumni platform

VIT Aero Alumni Connect is designed as a focused digital space for maintaining an active relationship between alumni, students and the Department of Aerospace Engineering.

Built for meaningful engagement

The portal gives the department a structured place to maintain alumni connections, communicate opportunities, recognise alumni contributions and create pathways for students to learn from experienced professionals.

It is designed to grow with the department — from a clean alumni directory today to a broader professional community over time.

The portal supports

  • Professional alumni profiles
  • Student-alumni interaction
  • Mentorship and career guidance
  • Internship and placement connections
  • Guest lectures and technical sessions
  • Alumni achievements
  • Department activities
  • Research collaboration
  • Industry connections
  • Project guidance and technical review
  • Higher-study interaction
  • Entrepreneurship guidance
